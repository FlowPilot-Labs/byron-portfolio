FleetPilot

A local-first trucking management prototype for dispatching loads, managing delivery documents, and coordinating owners, dispatchers, drivers, and customers — built for the web with Angular and IndexedDB.

Open live app Demo video

Problem

Small trucking operations often coordinate loads, drivers, delivery updates, and paperwork across calls, chat messages, spreadsheets, and paper documents. That makes it difficult to see where a load is, whether proof of delivery has been submitted, and which driver settlements are still pending.

Solution

FleetPilot brings the core shipment workflow into one focused web app. Dispatchers create and assign loads, move shipments through a visual dispatch board, and manage Rate Confirmations, Bills of Lading, and Proofs of Delivery. Owners get a clear operational view and can review simple driver settlements, while drivers and customers have role-specific portals for the information they need. Built as a local-first Angular prototype, FleetPilot stores demo data in IndexedDB so it remains usable without a backend.

Architecture

FleetPilot is a local-first Angular SPA with four role portals — owner, dispatcher, driver, and customer — sharing one shipment domain. Loads, drivers, vehicles, customers, documents (rate confirmations, bills of lading, proofs of delivery), settlements, and activity all live in the same browser store so every persona sees a consistent operational picture.

Dispatchers create and assign loads, then move them on a visual board with Material CDK drag-and-drop and status rules. Dexie wraps IndexedDB for persistence and seeds demo personas, loads, documents, and settlements on first launch — no sign-in backend required. Chart.js powers owner/dispatcher dashboard metrics. The app deploys to GitHub Pages; an ASP.NET Core API is reserved for future sync while the prototype stays fully client-side.
