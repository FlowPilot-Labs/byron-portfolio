FinDen

A personal finance app for tracking accounts, spending plans, and investments across Android and web — filler case study content for layout while the real story is written.

Open live app Demo video

Problem

Managing day-to-day money across multiple accounts often means juggling spreadsheets, bank apps, and mental notes. People need a clearer picture of balances, plans, and investments without stitching tools together by hand.

Solution

FinDen brings accounts, spending plans, and investments into one calm workspace. The focus is reliable offline-first tracking, simple money decisions, and a consistent experience on phone and web.

Architecture

High-level shape (filler): a shared domain model for accounts and transactions, local persistence for offline use, and a web client that mirrors the mobile flows. Sync and reporting details will be documented here later.
